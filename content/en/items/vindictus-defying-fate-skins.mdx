## Common Mistakes When Buying Vindictus Defying Fate Skins

Most regret stories follow the same three patterns.

- **Spreading hanner across multiple sets.** With a flat 2,200 price, partial spending rarely produces a complete look.
- **Ignoring challenge rewards.** Players who buy everything often discover later that a comparable cosmetic was available for free.
- **Buying for an alt before a main.** Your main character's outfit is the one you will see most often.

| Mistake | Cost | Better Approach |
|---|---|---|
| Buying two half-sets | Full price for two incomplete looks | Commit to one complete set |
| Skipping reward challenges | Wasted hanner on a duplicate style | Clear challenges first, then spend |
| Choosing purely on color | Mismatched weapon and armor tones | Match the set to your weapon's palette |
